# SignalSnagger 0.38.4e lockup-containment test candidate

This candidate retains the `0.38.4c` 48 ksample/s direct-listening path and
adds containment for the permanent UI/audio lockup observed with `0.38.4d`.

## Safety changes

- Count actual dropped 12 kHz DSP frames and consecutive backlog instead of
  treating an ADC ISR crossing the audio timer wrap as a missed deadline.
- After 64 consecutive dropped DSP frames (about 5.3 ms), center the DAC and
  stop the level-1 ADC event and interrupt source.
- Enable the AVR128DA48 hardware watchdog with a two-second period after normal
  startup.
- Feed the watchdog only from foreground code, and only after the switch/UI
  service and every expected audio stage have advanced since the prior feed.
- Preserve a validated audio-lockout marker and dropped-frame count across a
  watchdog reset in `.noinit` RAM without writing EEPROM.
- Replace the About-screen `A`/`T` row with `D<drops> W<reset>L<lockout>`:
  `D` is the actual dropped-frame count (capped at 999), `W1` reports that the
  current boot followed a watchdog reset, and `L1` reports that the audio
  backlog circuit breaker caused that reset.

The circuit breaker does not attempt partial audio recovery from interrupt
context. It releases the CPU and deliberately lets the independently clocked
watchdog restart the entire receiver, bounding the observed permanent-lockup
failure to approximately two seconds.

- Programmer: Atmel-ICE `J41800053674`; AVR128DA48 signature `1E9708`.
- Target voltage: 3.29 V before programming and 3.29 V after reset.
- Candidate HEX SHA-256:
  `bc56af5a6aee26cad095029490a8f392feafc613d729d0f269ab9783b35baf4b`.
- Full-flash readback SHA-256:
  `cd4d1b074a36b479427a6a2bb47b05fdac50705aa90a3d813c0d4ecc8ab6d300`.
- The independent 128 KiB flash readback matched the padded candidate image
  byte-for-byte.
- EEPROM, fuses, lock bits, and user row matched their pre-programming backups
  byte-for-byte.
- After a five-second post-reset interval and the subsequent UPDI attachment,
  a diagnostic SRAM snapshot showed watchdog-reset and audio-lockout flags
  clear, all three liveness heartbeats advancing, no consecutive backlog, and
  78 nonconsecutive dropped frames. Because debugger attachment can perturb
  real-time scheduling, the receiver was reset once more afterward and left at
  a clean boot; the undisturbed About-screen `D` result remains an operator
  check.

## Initial operator result (2026-11-02)

After receiver testing, the About screen reported `D999 W0L0`.

- At least 999 DSP frames were dropped; the display intentionally caps `D` at
  999.
- No watchdog reset occurred (`W0`).
- The 64-consecutive-frame audio backlog circuit breaker did not trip (`L0`),
  so successful DSP service occurred between the shorter drop bursts.

This result confirms that the permanent-lockup containment did not activate
during the test, but it also shows that the lower-priority DSP path still has
substantial scheduling loss. The operator confirmed that the UI remained
responsive and did not perceive discrete lost-frame gaps. The losses could not
be distinguished from the existing near-threshold noise. Headphone-removal
shutdown remains to be confirmed explicitly.
