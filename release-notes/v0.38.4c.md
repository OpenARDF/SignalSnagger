# SignalSnagger 0.38.4c diagnostic-display test candidate

This candidate preserves the `0.38.4b` direct-audio and interrupt-timing
behavior and changes only the About-screen display mode.

## User-visible change

- Restore the DOGS104 LCD to four-line mode when entering About from the
  three-line Main Menu, making the `A<m> T<t>` ADC deadline diagnostics visible.
- Retain the shortened `V:` prefix so the complete suffixed version fits its
  ten-column row.

`A` is the saturated ADC deadline-miss count, and `T` is the maximum measured
ADC ISR duration in ticks. The audio cadence timer period is 500 ticks.

## Programmer-side hardware validation

On 2026-11-01, this exact candidate was programmed into the connected
AVR128DA48 through Atmel-ICE `J41800053674` using a device-supported isolated
flash erase. The device signature was `1E9708`, and target voltage measured
3.28 V before programming.

- Firmware HEX SHA-256:
  `f7b4f6458f2b2f781aa7ee71349f0d5cf16212ebcbebe2154de146160e49eeca`
- Padded 128 KiB candidate and independent flash-readback SHA-256:
  `78da9d4966705a6c7526c60df32b85e4f9c3b01c5e7b5484733e11513a6a888d`
- Pymcuprog write verification passed.
- EEPROM, fuses, lock bits, and user row were byte-identical before and after
  programming.
- The target reset into normal execution and measured 3.27 V afterward.

The operator subsequently confirmed the complete `0.38.4c` suffix and reported
`A999 T260`. The timer-wrap counter therefore saturated, showing that the
48 ksample/s ADC ISR frequently crossed the audio timer's wrap point; this did
not by itself prove that the next ADC-result deadline was missed. The maximum
measured ISR body duration was 260 timer ticks. Other functional checks remain
pending.

After the rejected `0.38.4d` experiment locked up, this exact `0.38.4c` image
was restored on 2026-11-01. Its independent full-flash readback again had
SHA-256 `78da9d4966705a6c7526c60df32b85e4f9c3b01c5e7b5484733e11513a6a888d`.
EEPROM, fuses, lock bits, and user row matched their immediate pre-recovery
backups byte-for-byte. The target reset successfully and measured 3.29 V.
