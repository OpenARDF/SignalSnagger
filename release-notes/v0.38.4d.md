# SignalSnagger 0.38.4d 24 kHz direct-audio test candidate

This candidate tests whether recurring ADC interrupt deadline crossings account
for the remaining sensitivity loss in the direct listening path.

## Experimental change

- Reduce ADC capture and direct DAC output from 48 to 24 ksample/s.
- Change decimation from four-to-one to two-to-one so the detector and filtered
  audio paths remain at their established 12 ksample/s rate.
- Preserve the `0.38.4c` direct conversion, gain, cue timing, detector, filter,
  and deadline instrumentation behavior.

The audio cadence timer period increases from 500 to 1000 CPU ticks. CW audio
and user-interface cues remain far below the resulting 12 kHz Nyquist limit.
The expected About-screen result is `A0`, with `T` comfortably below 1000.

## Programmer validation (2026-11-01)

- Programmer: Atmel-ICE `J41800053674`; AVR128DA48 signature `1E9708`.
- Target voltage: 3.27 V before programming and 3.29 V after reset.
- Candidate HEX SHA-256:
  `0a69fc391bc7c4068943905b4883a62360d3841bf75a8c847604489017781465`.
- Full-flash readback SHA-256:
  `94c766a83bc8b0b886953f1c43641bf0a9978548ea3ab15c6da7ba738ce46a1b`.
- The independent 128 KiB flash readback matched the padded candidate image
  byte-for-byte.
- EEPROM, fuses, lock bits, and user row matched their pre-programming backups
  byte-for-byte.
- The receiver was reset successfully after verification.

## Operator result

- The About screen reported `A0 T256` on the receiver.
- No ADC deadline crossings were initially observed at the 24 ksample/s
  cadence.
- The maximum measured ADC ISR duration was approximately 10.7 microseconds
  at the 24 MHz CPU clock, essentially unchanged from the prior candidate.
- Listening sensitivity was approximately the same as `0.38.4c`.
- During the test, the UI and headphone-removal handling became unresponsive
  while audio continued with artifacts. Removing the headphones no longer
  initiated shutdown or reset, leaving the receiver unusable without the
  programmer.

Before recovery, a full 12 KiB SRAM capture was preserved. Symbols mapped
against the exact candidate showed:

- ADC deadline-miss count: 65,535 (saturated).
- Maximum ADC ISR timer measurement: 914 ticks.
- Dropped DSP-frame count: 60,310.
- Audio-frame-ready flag: set, with a pending sample.
- Headphone state: still detected.

These values show that the initial `A0` observation did not persist. Audio/DSP
service had fallen severely behind, and the lower-priority UI/headphone path
was no longer progressing while high-priority audio activity continued.

## Disposition

This candidate is rejected. It provided no sensitivity benefit and introduced
an unacceptable lockup. The receiver was restored to the independently
verified `0.38.4c` image; EEPROM, fuses, lock bits, and user row were preserved.
The working source was immediately returned to the `0.38.4c` 48 ksample/s
constants before subsequent lockup-containment work began.
